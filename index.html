Chapter One

Two instruments, two continents, one conversation.

The Atlas Duo brings together the dark warmth of viola and the percussive clarity of guitar — an unusual pairing that opens new ground in chamber music. Across borders, climates, and time zones, the two voices trade lines like travelers swapping stories at a table.

Our journey traces the spine of the Americas: from the Florida coast up the eastern seaboard, across the upper Midwest into the Canadian Rockies, down the Pacific through Mexico and Central America, and through the whole of South America — Andes, Patagonia, the Atlantic side back up through Brazil and the Guianas. Along the way: thirty-six concerts, three languages, twenty countries, countless borrowed kitchens.

Martín · Viola
Viola

Martín Rojas

Colombian-American · raised in Washington DC

Colombian-American violist Martín Rojas was born and raised in Washington DC, a graduate of the Manhattan School of Music where he studied with Juilliard String Quartet violist Molly Carr and New York Philharmonic assistant principal Cong Wu.

Martín began his viola studies at fourteen with Pedro Romero of the Royal Seville Symphony Orchestra. His passion for the instrument grew quickly, and a year later he entered the Peabody Preparatory's Performance Academy for Strings under the tutelage of Dr. Christian Tremblay.

In the years since, Martín has participated in the Curtis Summerfest Young Artists Program, the Aspen Music Festival and School, Domaine Forget, and Virtuoso and Belcanto. In 2022 he attended the Järna Festival Academy in Sweden, performing chamber music alongside Eivind Ringstad and Jennifer Stumm. He has given recitals at Carnegie Hall, the Peabody Institute, the Eastman School of Music, and overseas in Colombia and Sweden.

In recent years he has worked in masterclasses with internationally renowned violists including Bruno Giuranna, Roberto Díaz, Antoine Tamestit, and Nobuko Imai. In 2024 he was awarded the Stephen and Elaine Scholarship from the New York Philharmonic.
